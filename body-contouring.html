Ultrasonic cavitation

Ultrasound energy targets fat cells under the skin in a specific area, like the belly, flanks or thighs. It's a non-surgical way to work on localized fat and the look of cellulite.

RF skin tightening

Radiofrequency gently heats the deeper layers of the skin to encourage new collagen. Over time, skin looks firmer and tighter.

Who it's for

Body contouring works best on small areas of stubborn fat that don't respond to diet and exercise, or on loose skin after weight loss. It's not a weight-loss treatment.

Body contouring questions

Is this surgery?

No. Cavitation and RF are both non-surgical, with no needles and no downtime. Most clients go straight back to their day.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the area and your goals. Sessions are sold one at a time or as a 6-session package, and results vary by person.

Does it hurt?

Most clients find it comfortable. Cavitation can make a buzzing sound, and RF feels warm.
