How it works

A probe finer than the hair itself slides into the follicle, right alongside the hair. A tiny current treats the cells that grow the hair, and the hair lifts out with no pulling.

Because electrolysis goes straight to the follicle, it doesn't depend on pigment the way laser does. Hair color and skin tone don't matter, which is why it works where laser can't.

Each follicle is treated one by one, so it's best for smaller areas and for finishing what laser leaves behind.

What to expect

Each follicle needs treating while its hair is growing, so results come over a series of visits.

  1. Free consult

    Crystal looks at your hair and skin, tells you whether electrolysis, laser or both makes sense, and sets a plan.

  2. Before each session

    Don't shave, wax or pluck the area beforehand. The hair needs to be long enough to see. Crystal will tell you how long to let it grow.

  3. The session

    Most clients describe it as a brief warm pinch at each follicle. Sessions run from 15 to 90 minutes, depending on the area.

  4. Over time

    Hair grows in cycles, so new hairs surface between visits. Regular sessions catch each one until the area is clear for good.

Electrolysis questions

Is it really permanent?

Yes. Electrolysis treats each follicle directly, and a follicle that's fully treated doesn't grow hair again. It's the only method recognized as permanent hair removal.

Does it work on dark skin?

Yes. Electrolysis doesn't rely on pigment, so skin tone doesn't change how it works. It's safe and effective on every skin type.

Does it work on gray or blonde hair?

Yes. Because it treats the follicle directly instead of the pigment in the hair, electrolysis works on gray, white, blonde, red and fine hair that laser can't see.

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe it as a brief warm pinch at each follicle. Sensitivity varies by area and person.

Can I do laser and electrolysis together?

Yes. Many clients use laser to clear larger areas of dark hair, then electrolysis to finish the light or fine hairs laser leaves behind.
