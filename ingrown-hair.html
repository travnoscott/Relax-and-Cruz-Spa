Why it keeps happening

Shaving and waxing leave a sharp tip on the hair. Curly or coarse hair bends back as it grows and pushes into the skin beside it. Your skin treats it like a splinter, and you get a bump.

It's most common on curly hair and darker skin, and a closer shave usually makes it worse.

Why laser helps

Laser treats the follicle itself. Over a series of sessions there's less hair, and finer hair, so there's less left to curl back in.

Ingrown hair questions

Can laser really help razor bumps?

Yes. Razor bumps come from hair growing back into the skin. Laser treats the follicle, so over a series of sessions there's less hair, and finer hair, left to grow back in.

Is it safe on dark skin?

Yes, with the right laser. The Nd:YAG's longer wavelength passes through skin pigment to reach the follicle, and it's the standard choice for deeper skin.

How many sessions does it take?

Most clients need 8 to 12 sessions, spaced 6 to 8 weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles.

What should I do between sessions?

Shave the area the day before each visit. Skip waxing, plucking and tanning between sessions, since laser needs the hair's root in place.

What about the dark marks?

Those are a separate concern from the hair. Facials and peels can help even out tone, and Crystal can suggest a plan for both at your free consult.
