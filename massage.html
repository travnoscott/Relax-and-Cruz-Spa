Massage questions

What's the difference between Swedish and deep tissue?

Swedish massage uses lighter pressure and long, gliding strokes to relax the whole body. Deep tissue uses firmer pressure to work on knots.

Which length should I book?

30 minutes suits a quick back and shoulder session. 60 or 90 minutes covers the full body.
